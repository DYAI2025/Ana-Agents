## 4. Drei englische Pitches (je <100 Wörter)
### Plumbline
Plumbline is an early-stage reliability framework for agentic software delivery. Its executable integrity checks distinguish passing tests from sufficient evidence about the intended requirement. We have locally verified the narrow deterministic gate, not a universal guarantee of semantic correctness. We are seeking engineering teams for a paid evaluation on one bounded workflow: can explicit evidence requirements expose unsupported completion claims before release? The next milestone is a reproducible comparison against the team's existing review process, including false positives and review cost.

### ASM
ASM is an early-stage story-mapping tool built around a canonical, agent-readable product file. It connects goals, personas, needs, narrative steps and explicit human approval. Its domain and workflow tests pass locally; live model quality and customer outcomes remain unverified. We are looking for product teams to evaluate one discovery-to-engineering handoff, measuring whether reviewers can recover the original intent and identify missing requirements. The immediate offer is a scoped design partnership, not a finished enterprise collaboration platform.

### PXAPI
PXAPI is an early-stage website-analysis engine that links diagnostic findings to evidence and measurements. Its code separates an observed website issue from a failed measurement, so a timeout should not become a negative verdict about the business. We are seeking an agency partner for a paid, bounded evaluation of static-page diagnosis. The local demo still needs a compatible runtime; no production or conversion-impact claim is being made. The commercial question is whether evidence-backed reports reduce manual verification enough to support repeatable paid delivery.
