# Genericness and Follow-up

## Genericness

A draft must depend materially on Brand-specific evidence. If replacing the Brand with a plausible competitor leaves the substantive proposition intact, revise or return NOT_READY. Brand-name insertion alone is not specificity.

## Follow-up

Without a new signal, do not invent urgency, traction, events, relationship context, or new factual claim IDs. A follow-up may restate the existing proposition more concisely.
