# Evidence and Framing

Every material Brand/Creator statement in the draft must map to a validated upstream claim ID.

- FACT: may be ASSERTED, HEDGED, or QUESTION.
- SUPPORTED_INFERENCE: HEDGED or QUESTION only.
- HYPOTHESIS: HEDGED or QUESTION only.
- UNKNOWN: forbidden from external use.

Unsupported factual content makes the draft NOT_READY.

A clean schema proves contract consistency, not truth.
